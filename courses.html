Our Climbing Courses

Alpine Ascents offers three focused programs. Each course has its own skill path, duration, and price tier so beginners and experienced climbers can train at the right level.

Ice Climbing

Ice Climbing teaches you how to move on frozen waterfalls, couloirs, and glacier ice using crampons, ice tools, and ropes. You learn ice-screw placement, belaying on ice, reading ice quality, and how to stay safe in cold, steep terrain.

This course is for climbers who already hike in snow or want to move from rock into winter alpine routes. Guides cover falling ice, cold injuries, and efficient swinging technique so you can climb with control, not just power.

  • Crampons & ice tools
  • Ice screws & anchors
  • Glacier ice travel

Prices: Intro (2 days) — $450  |  Intermediate (4 days) — $850  |  Advanced (7 days) — $1,450

Rock Climbing

Rock Climbing builds movement, rope work, and outdoor judgement on real rock. You start with belaying, knots, and top-rope technique, then move into lead climbing, natural protection, and multi-pitch systems used on mountain walls.

The course covers communication, fall management, and how to choose safe routes. It is the best first step before alpine climbing, because rock skills are the foundation of almost every mountain day.

  • Top-rope & lead
  • Belay & anchors
  • Multi-pitch systems

Prices: Intro (1 day) — $180  |  Outdoor skills (3 days) — $520  |  Lead / multi-pitch (5 days) — $890

Alpine Skills

Alpine Skills brings hiking, rock, snow, and ice together for real mountain travel. You learn glacier travel, rope team movement, crevasse rescue, navigation, weather reading, and how to move efficiently above the treeline.

This is the course for people who want to join expeditions or climb mixed routes. Training focuses on decision-making: when to turn around, how to manage time, and how a small team stays safe on big terrain.

  • Glacier & rope team
  • Navigation & weather
  • Expedition habits

Prices: Foundation (5 days) — $1,200  |  Complete (8 days) — $1,950  |  Expedition prep (12 days) — $2,850
