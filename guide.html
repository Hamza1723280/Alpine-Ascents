Trip Guidelines

Alpine Ascents is a project-based adventure website. These guidelines explain how our trips, courses, and mountain days are planned so every climber knows what to expect before they join.

1. Fitness and honesty

Tell us your real climbing level when you book. Ice Climbing, Rock Climbing, and Alpine Skills are different loads. If you have not used a harness or crampons before, start with the intro course. A truthful fitness note helps our instructors keep the group safe and moving at a fair pace.

Walk, stair training, and light pack carries for two to three weeks before a course. You do not need to be an athlete, but you should be ready for long days on your feet.

2. What to bring

01

Clothing

Layered clothes work best: a base layer, a warm mid layer, and a waterproof shell. Avoid cotton on mountain days. Bring gloves, a warm hat, sunglasses, and spare socks.

02

Personal kit

Bring your ID, any regular medicine, sunscreen, a 1–2 litre water bottle, and a small headlamp. If you own climbing shoes or boots, bring them. Technical gear is provided on courses.

03

Documents

Carry a printed or phone copy of your booking name, emergency contact, and any doctor note for asthma, allergies, or past injuries. Instructors need this before the first briefing.

3. Safety on the mountain

Follow the instructor’s rope commands. Do not walk above other climbers on loose rock. Stay clipped in until you are told to unclip. On ice and glacier days, never step off the marked line without a rope team.

Weather can stop a plan. If wind, lightning, or avalanche risk is high, we turn around. Reaching the top is a goal, not a rule. Coming back together is the standard we use for every Alpine Ascents trip.

4. Booking, payment, and cancellation

  • Book through Contact Us with your course name
  • 50% advance to hold your seat
  • Balance due 7 days before the start date
  • Cancel 10+ days before: 80% refund
  • Cancel 3–9 days before: 40% refund
  • Cancel under 3 days: no refund

If Alpine Ascents cancels because of weather or instructor shortage, you get a full refund or a free move to the next date.

5. Group rules

Respect the team

Courses run in small groups. Listen during briefings, keep phones on silent on the wall, and help pack shared ropes. No alcohol or smoking on climbing days.

Leave no trace

Carry all wrappers back. Do not break plants, stack extra cairns, or leave chalk marks on rock. Mountains around our training areas stay open only if we treat them well.

Age and consent

Minimum age is 16 with a parent or guardian form. Ages 18+ can join independently. All climbers sign a simple risk acknowledgement before the first session.

6. How to join

Open the Courses page, choose Ice Climbing, Rock Climbing, or Alpine Skills, then send the Contact Us form with your dates and experience. Our team replies with gear list, meeting point, and start time.

Questions? Write to us from the Contact Us page. Karachi office: C, 21, Lucknow Society, Sector 31 E Korangi.
